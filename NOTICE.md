# In the Spirit of My Tools

## Why I Share My Work

These tools represent considerable time, experimentation, development and creative effort.

I have chosen to release them as open-source software because I believe useful technology should be able to grow beyond its original purpose, benefit other developers and artists, and continue evolving long after its initial release.

Although many of these tools originated from my work with DAZ Studio, they have deliberately been designed as standalone applications. I hope they will find uses throughout the wider 3D community.

## Freedom to Build Upon My Work

I welcome individuals, independent developers and commercial organisations using, modifying, extending and integrating these tools into their own workflows and applications.

I encourage improvements to be shared so that the wider community can benefit from them.

My choice of the GNU Affero General Public License reflects my commitment to keeping this work accessible and ensuring that improvements to the covered software remain available under its terms.

## Recognition of Original Developers

Open-source development does not happen without people investing their time, knowledge and effort.

For independent developers, recognition can be particularly important. It helps establish professional reputations, creates opportunities for future work and encourages continued contributions.

I ask those who incorporate my tools into other applications to acknowledge their origins in a meaningful and reasonably discoverable way.

I do not expect my original interface, branding or presentation to be preserved. I simply hope that users can readily discover who originally developed the technology they are using.

Where practical, I encourage attribution through an application's normal About, Credits or Legal Notices functionality, rather than solely through files hidden within an installation directory.

## Collaboration and Compatibility

My tools are intended to work with other software, not exist in isolation.

I encourage developers creating bridges, plugins and integrations to publish their source code and document their interfaces, allowing improvements to benefit the original project, its forks and other applications.

I recognise that different applications have different technical and licensing requirements. My aim is to encourage cooperation rather than unnecessarily restrict integration.

## A Shared Future

I hope these tools will become more capable, more accessible and more useful through the contributions of others.

I welcome forks, adaptations and new applications built upon my work.

I ask only that those benefiting from the work remember the people who made it possible, and extend that same consideration to those who contribute in the future.
